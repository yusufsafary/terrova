Tokenomics

One supply, flat claims

$TERRA is a fixed-supply ERC-20 on BNB Chain. Total supply is 10,000,000,000 TERRA, minted once at deploy to the project wallet. No further minting exists in the contract.

Claim math

  • One plot claims 100,000 TERRA once. All 2,500 plots unmerged would pay 250,000,000 TERRA.
  • One estate claims 300,000 TERRA once. If every plot merged into 1,250 estates, claims would pay 375,000,000 TERRA.
  • The 400,000,000 TERRA claim pool covers both extremes in full.
  • Claim fee is a flat 0.0003 BNB per claim, forwarded to the project wallet inside the same transaction.
  • Unclaimed TERRA burns when the pool launches on 1 December 2026.

Plot pricing

  • First plot per wallet: free, gated by Agent ID ownership plus an anti-bot question.
  • Plots 2 to 10 per wallet: 0.0002 BNB each, forwarded to the project wallet inside the same transaction.
  • Hard cap: 10 plots minted per wallet, 2,500 plots in the world.
