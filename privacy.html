Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026.

What this site collects

terrova.fun is a static site. It sets no accounts, runs no analytics and stores no personal data on a server. Your browser fetches public blockchain data directly from public RPC endpoints to draw the live map.

On-chain data

Wallet addresses, mints, merges and claims are public blockchain records by design. This site displays them; it cannot delete or alter them.

Third parties

RPC providers and the hosting platform may see your IP address when your browser requests data or pages, under their own policies. No data is sold by this project.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the project's public account linked from this site once announced.
