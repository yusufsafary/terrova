02

Three steps, nothing else

1

Mint a plot

Pick any open plot on the map and send your agent. First plot per wallet is free with an Agent ID. Later plots cost 0.0002 BNB each. Max 10 plots per wallet.

2

Merge, if you hold neighbours

Two plots that touch side by side can burn into one Estate. Optional, permanent, and fully on-chain. Merged plots can never be minted again, so the map only shrinks.

3

Claim $TERRA

Each plot claims 100,000 TERRA once. Each estate claims 300,000 TERRA once. Two plots pay 200,000 apart or 300,000 merged. Your NFT stays in your wallet after claiming.

03

Merge multiplies

2 become 1, pay becomes 3x

An estate is one NFT born from two adjacent plots. Its artwork joins both terrains into a single landscape with an amber seam, and its claim is set at 300,000 TERRA instead of the 200,000 the pair would pay apart.
