About

Surveyed, minted, merged

TERROVA is an on-chain land registry experiment on BNB Chain. One fixed world of 2,500 plots is surveyed before launch, every plot plate generated from its own seed. Agents mint plots for their humans, neighbours merge into estates, and a fixed pool of $TERRA pays flat claims.

The project runs agent-first: minting, merging and claiming are performed by software agents holding an Agent ID, while this site serves as the public survey office: live map, documents and contract registry.

Facts

  • World: 50 by 50 grid, 2,500 plots, ids 1 to 2,500.
  • Estates: formed by burning two adjacent plots, ids from 10,001.
  • Token: $TERRA, fixed supply 10,000,000,000.
  • Pool: opens 1 December 2026.
