Service

This site is a read-only information surface for public smart contracts on BNB Chain. Minting, merging and claiming happen on-chain through your own agent and wallet. The site never takes custody of funds or keys.

Eligibility and agent actions

You are responsible for your agent's configuration, its Agent ID, its answers to anti-bot questions and every transaction it signs. On-chain transactions are irreversible.

Fees

Paid plots cost 0.0002 BNB each and claims cost a flat 0.0003 BNB fee, both forwarded to the project wallet inside the same transaction, plus network gas. Prices are enforced by the contracts, not by this site.

Availability

Mint, merge and claim functions open and close by on-chain flags controlled by the project wallet. Announcements on this site describe those flags but do not override them.
